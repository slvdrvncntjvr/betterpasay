# Pasay research and portal scope

Research date: **28 October 2026 (Asia/Manila)**. This records evidence and limits, not a certification of government data accuracy.

## What the portal should contain

Prioritize resident tasks: find a service, reach original guidance, and inspect public records. The redesigned portal covers Home, Services, Our City, Government, Barangays, Documents, Transparency, Contacts and About, with the community maintainer and correction process visible. It does not collect applications or personal records.

The original review below is supplemented by [verified 2026 charter sections and document links](PASAY-DOCUMENTS.md), [implemented service summaries](SERVICE-EXPANSION.md), [city, government and barangay evidence](PASAY-SOURCES.md), and [five-portal design research](DESIGN-RESEARCH.md). The current implementation contains 21 services, 25 document records, eight transparency resources and all 201 PSA barangay records.

The [official Pasay homepage](https://pasay.gov.ph/) links resident PDFs, online systems, a charter and transparency resources. The [city service page](https://pasay.gov.ph/serbisyo/) identifies permit/tax portals. Individual sources below were checked before summaries were written.

## Unresolved leads and omissions

- A complete Pasay-specific COA annual audit collection was not verified. Searches also returned **Pasig**, which must not be confused with Pasay. No unrelated city audit or clean-audit claim is included.
- The procurement-reports subpage timed out; the portal uses reviewed procurement indexes instead.
- Priority civil registry, health, PWD, solo-parent and medical-assistance sections have been reviewed and implemented with edition/page references. The rest of the 1,193-page charter remains outside the reviewed scope. Later advisories still need periodic checks; never borrow another LGU’s requirements.
- Published emergency numbers are now included with official sources and review notes. No test calls were made; a published listing does not prove current operation.
- The official homepage labels EMI portal coming soon. It is not advertised as working.
- 2024 census populations are now included from PSA records; the city’s 2020 figure is separately dated. Barangay hall contacts and district/zone mappings remain unverified. No project-progress claims, endorsements or formal partnerships are asserted.

## Verification model

The service/document registry is `src/data/sources.json`; city, leadership, contact and barangay evidence is in `src/data/city.json`. `reviewed` means supporting sections/pages read, with scope described. `linked` means identity/provenance checked with limited content review. Both include actual review dates. Unknown `publishedAt` is intentionally `null`. Editions and reporting periods are separate.

External availability checks are diagnostics, not evidence that a source is accurate. Do not advance content-review dates on HTTP success alone.
