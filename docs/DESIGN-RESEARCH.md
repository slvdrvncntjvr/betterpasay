# Better Pasay design research

Checked **28 October 2026 (Asia/Manila)**. Research covers the live BetterLGU guidance, five requested portals and official Pasay sources. Recommendations below are editorial/design judgments, not measured usability findings. No source code was changed for this research.

## Guidance and scope

The [BetterLGU directory](https://lgu.bettergov.ph/) is a community-maintained index of portals, repositories, maintainers and maintenance states. Its [starting guide](https://lgu.bettergov.ph/GUIDE) asks builders to find existing efforts before duplicating them, publish public information with an independent civic identity, use a public repository, and maintain the information. It suggests periodic reviews and co-maintainers. Apply that here through visible community ownership, original sources, a correction route and an honest coverage statement. Listing or launch announcements require a separate maintainer action; this research did not submit anything externally.

## What was actually observed

All five homepages were opened in headless Chromium at 1440 × 1000 and visually inspected. Additional service pages were rendered at 390 × 844 for Los Baños, Bacolod and Makati; those three sampled pages had no document-level horizontal overflow. This is a small layout sample, not an accessibility audit. Text-only browsing could read Las Piñas and Solano but could not extract useful content from the other three, so their observations come from real browser rendering.

## Suggested Pasay composition

Use the implementation's navy/white/yellow identity consistently: navy for headings and the service hero, white/light neutral surfaces for reading, yellow for one primary action. If a city photograph is used, keep the text/search on a predictable opaque or strongly shaded surface and record the photograph's permission/credit. This palette and composition are proposed choices, not an official Pasay identity specification.

Suggested page order:

1. Compact brand/navigation with “Independent community guide”, an official Pasay website link and a visible mobile menu.
2. Short task-led heading, one-line explanation and a labelled search field. Suggested placeholder: “Birth certificate, PWD ID, sedula…”. Suggested shortcuts must resolve to published, sourced entries.
3. Six category cards: Civil registry & documents; Family & assistance; Health; Business & property; Work; City inquiries. On desktop use three columns; on phones one or two depending on title length.
4. A small documents/public-records area, with explicit distinction between service guides, downloadable forms and transparency records.
5. Source-check explanation, correction link and community-maintainer footer.

Recommended implementation targets: roughly 1120–1200 px content width, 16 px minimum body text, 24–32 px card padding, 44 px interactive targets, visible focus, persistent field labels and text with icons. Keep results usable without imagery. Use static content before maps/weather/news; those extras should not delay the resident task list. These numbers are proposed acceptance targets, not measurements of the reference sites.

## Gaps in the initial Pasay data

The baseline `src/data/services.json` reviewed for this task had **11 entries**: senior ID, cedula, mayor's clearance, business permit, building permit, property tax, cremation, jobs, PESO, procurement forms and city inquiries. `src/data/sources.json` already carried source format/check dates and distinguished reviewed sources from merely linked documents. Preserve that useful evidence model.

| Priority | Content/design improvement                                                                                                               | Evidence and publishing condition                                                                                                                                                  |
| -------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| First    | Add civil registry, PWD, solo parent and health tasks. Use resident language and Filipino search aliases.                                | Verified official references and selected requirements are in [PASAY-DOCUMENTS.md](PASAY-DOCUMENTS.md). Choose the appropriate applicant case before expanding a checklist.        |
| First    | Create a local documents collection with filter labels such as “Service guide”, “Application form” and “Public record”.                  | The [official forms index](https://pasay.gov.ph/serbisyo/downloadable-forms/) currently exposes procurement documents. Its title alone does not establish a resident form library. |
| First    | Make source detail actionable: title → short scope → office → selected requirements → original source/download → checked date.           | A source PDF is not an online application. Buttons should say “Read official guide (PDF)” or “Download procurement form”, as applicable.                                           |
| Next     | Separate record copies from new registration and corrections; separate a health certificate for employment from medical care/assistance. | Similar keywords describe materially different errands. Use clarifying descriptions and cross-links instead of a single vague “Certificates” result.                               |
| Next     | Add usable empty results and aliases: `kapanganakan`, `kasal`, `kamatayan`, `PWD`, `solo parent`, `health certificate`, `sedula`.        | These are search suggestions, not asserted official translations. Empty results should link to official city inquiries and explain missing coverage.                               |
| Ongoing  | Show edition, source-check date and coverage limits independently.                                                                       | A date inside an upload path is not a publication date. A linked file is not automatically a fully reviewed file, and an older PDF can differ from the current charter.            |

For review, check the actual tasks “find the PWD guide”, “find a birth-record copy”, “download a real blank form”, and “understand who maintains this site” on a narrow phone viewport. Ensure official links remain ordinary accessible links, long document names wrap, and changing a category leaves a clear result count and reset action. Do not reuse the reference portals' contacts, financial figures or benefit claims as Pasay content.
