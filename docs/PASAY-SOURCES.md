The 201 rows were extracted from the PSA's full city table, with a sequential-number and unique-code check. Their 2024 populations total **453,186**, exactly matching the city total. The barangay page includes all records in static HTML and adds exact-number and code search as progressive enhancement. Its number ranges are explicitly labeled browsing shortcuts, not administrative groupings. Each record links to the corresponding `https://psa.gov.ph/classification/psgc/brgydetail/{code}` destination exposed by the PSA table. Individual PSA pages can retain older data; the displayed 2024 population comes from the citywide table.

PSA's page identifies its data and content as **CC BY 4.0**, unless otherwise stated. Attribution to the Philippine Statistics Authority and the [license](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) is present on the public barangay page.

**Missing data is explicit:** barangay hall addresses, direct telephone numbers, current barangay officials, hours and fees were not verified and were not invented. Zone 20 for Barangay 201 is the one individual zone assignment published here because it is directly established by the linked law. Full local zone/district mapping remains an editorial follow-up requiring an inspectable current official table or map; residents can still navigate every officially listed barangay now.

## Retrieval notes

Official sources were inspected through web browsing. Direct Python HTTP requests to PSA and Pasay returned HTTP 403, while the browser/search service could read their public indexed pages. The 2026 Citizen's Charter PDF was discoverable, but opening the full PDF exceeded the browser tool's content-size limit (28,493,768 bytes); its snippets were research leads only, and its uninspected full contents are not treated as verified evidence for these pages. The old official heritage book contains dated official lists and was not used for current officeholders, barangay contacts or service requirements. Some old `/our-government/` links redirect to `/pamahalaan/`; this implementation uses the reviewed current paths.
